# What this adds to the thesis

**The strongest finding here is a measurement/mechanism result: accepted
news-response orders can exaggerate, hide or reverse what actually executes.**
This is a retrospective replay of36 EXISTING simple-agent replies. No new
model responses were obtained. It strengthens an explanatory thesis example;
it does not complete the failed fresh-state R033 study or validate humans.

## Three useful cases, with the awkward results retained

1. **A positive response can mean less selling.** Arjun/2882's no-headline
   replies sell100, sell80, hold. Positive-headline replies all hold. Original
   accepted contrasts are+100,+80,0(mean+60 shares). In the original book both
   market sells fill21, so replayed contrasts are+21,+21,0(mean+14), with no buy.
   Counting only buy decisions would miss it; using accepted size overstates
   its executed magnitude. The corresponding conditional price differences
   are+4.9,+4.9,0 currency units, not a full public-news market result.

2. **Even the mean direction can change between measures.** In the same state,
   negative-headline orders are hold, market-sell100, limit-sell100 at98.0.
   Relative to the silent replies, accepted contrasts are+100,-20,-100
   (mean-6.67). Counterfactual fills give+21,0,0(mean+7). Both market-sell80 and
   market-sell100 meet only21 shares of executable opposing demand; the98.0
   sell does not fill when that auction clears97.6. This is a nonlinear
   quote/liquidity effect on THESE recorded draws, not proof that negative
   news makes the model optimistic or prices generally rise.

3. **An executed null can coexist with a feasible sell decision.** Meera/1441
   owns70 shares. All three negative-headline replies request10-share sells,
   but none fills in the original book. One ask is108.0, eligible at the108.9
   clearing price, yet14 other shares have better ask priority and exhaust
   the14-share demand. This is not an unaffordable/impossible sell or simply
   a limit outside the clearing price. With ONLY current MM quote size doubled
   from10 to20, that very same order fills10. Resources, spread/skew/cap,
   histories, other orders and target replies stay fixed. This is a controlled
   within-model book-depth mechanism, not new agent behaviour or calibrated
   liquidity. The other two higher asks still do not fill.

## The less tidy cases matter too

Meera/2882's positive contrasts remain mixed: accepted-50,-30,+40 and
replayed fills-2,-10,+12. Mean filled contrast is0 despite three nonzero
contrasts. The deeper-MM book gives-2,-20,+22, still mean0. Do not turn the
average into a claim that every reply ignores news. Arjun/1441 holds in all
9 A-only headline/repeat queries despite192 sellable shares; replay adds no
new action. Meera/2882's negative mean fills are-4.67(original) and-8.00
(deeper). All signs and contexts are in the readout, not only textbook cases.

The Arjun/2882 accepted-versus-filled mean sign reversal persists in the one
deeper-book check(-6.67 accepted versus+10.33 filled); positive mean fills
increase from14 to20.67. Magnitudes and some zero/nonzero outcomes depend on
liquidity. There is no basis for selecting quote_size20 as more realistic or
changing the production default. This check was selected POST-HOC after the
base result and changes the CURRENT book only, not its prior market history.

## How much can we claim?

All36 original A replies and exact prompts were audited; both original source
markets replay price-by-price and trade-by-trade, including allocation RNG.
Accepted quantities are unchanged; experiment-specific IDs are explicitly
mapped back to the source accounts. Source auction/accounting code hashes
match current code. All36 original-book and36 deeper-book conditional auctions
settle under the real simulator functions and pass cash/share/MM checks.
Three same-order payout/benchmark variants are accounting counterfactuals,
not extra draws. Both one-auction stay-put marks and counterpart outcomes are
preserved, including negatives; neither mark measures risk-adjusted skill.

These four agent frames come from ONLY TWO reused markets. Three previously
generated replies per condition do not become new independent evidence by
replaying them. One focal agent's order changes; the other13 traders' orders
stay fixed, including the other LLM's response. This is a partial one-auction
intervention, not simultaneous public-news feedback, lasting price discovery,
personality causality or human validation. Original4096/v2 shown-length query
conditions remain distinct from the unrun8192/common-hidden R033 trials.

## Decision

Promote execution/opportunity measurement as a stronger THESIS CANDIDATE, with
this figure and the one disclosed robustness check. Keep background trade-offs
and component/context/cost as the other candidates. Preserve selectable A/B/C
and original defaults. Next confirmation is fresh matched-state news data with
actual execution and eventually joint/sequential feedback, once delivery works.
Do not spend another loop on horizon cues, automatic reflection, tuning wording
to force expected signs or provider quality claims from timeouts.

R033 remains stopped with zero completed source rounds/headline trials. Its
two timed-out HTTP attempts and one blocked request are preserved separately;
the planned fresh study is implemented/offline checked but behaviourally unrun.
There is no API process running and no automatic restart scheduled.
