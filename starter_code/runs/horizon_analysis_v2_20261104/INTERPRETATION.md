## Primary result and its limits

The complete positive-shock pair(seed7403) has **zero executed shares in the
last three rounds for both actors in both conditions**: twelve HOLD choices,
zero gross trade, and zero shown-minus-hidden net-flow contrast. Shown-length
agents explicitly refer to the last/final rounds while holding. Thus deadline
language in reasoning is not sufficient evidence of liquidation. This one
scenario does not establish equivalence or rule out a horizon effect elsewhere;
the original A6317 exit remains an unresolved, context-specific lead.

Earlier trades and financial outcomes differ. Meera's shown-minus-hidden
passive excess is+1.96/+1.80 percentage points under auction/benchmark marks;
Arjun's is+1.26/+1.24. These are full-path descriptive differences, not a late
liquidation effect or proof that showing the endpoint improves decisions.
Pre-news states already diverge, and there is only one provider draw/condition.
The post-event gap is4.84% shown versus4.42% hidden; both last3 price returns
are positive. Profits, smaller gaps and hidden endpoints are not realism scores.

The downside shown-length arm stopped at12 rounds when Arjun's round13 call
and its identical-message extra attempt both timed out after180seconds. The
primary downside contrast and24-round wealth outcomes remain **missing**.
Meera's valid13 HOLD was delivered but never executed or fed back. Do not
substitute a12-round cutoff or retry/splice this failed path for a nicer result.

## Useful broader evidence: intentions, opportunity and execution

In the completed downside hidden-length path(seed8517), Arjun owned194 shares
before the event. He held at11-13, then requested sells at14-21 and actually
sold all194 by21. Total post-event requests were686 shares, accepted663,
filled194; those are repeated attempts to sell a shrinking position, not686
distinct shares or663 executed sells. Round14's100-share request filled16;
round15's100 filled25. This is a useful execution/liquidity illustration.

Meera held73 shares throughout the post-event period. Her later reasoning
mentions reluctance to realize losses; Arjun describes broken momentum.
Those are model self-descriptions, not established human biases or causal
trait effects: profiles bundle several traits and their preceding portfolios
differ. Both initially acknowledge the dividend-cut news but give it little
valuation weight. Arjun's later sales therefore cannot be called a clean
immediate headline response; forecast and actual dividend change are bundled,
and prices, other traders and own histories evolve together.

Meera's downside passive excess is+4.82/+4.17pp, Arjun's-8.00/-10.40pp under
the two marks. Meera sold27 shares BEFORE the event; holding after it should
not be credited as the source of that relative gain. Arjun is already out of
shares by21, so his last3 HOLDs do not mean no downside response. This is why
the primary late-window statistic must sit beside the full executed trajectory.

These are exploratory measurement examples, not a second completed horizon
comparison, isolated news/trait causality, skill ranking or human validation.

## Next research priority

The existing environment checkpoint demonstrates a real within-model trade-off:
averaging dividends increases return dependence and reduces turnover; choosing
the lowest valuation gap would conceal these costs. Retain skew.02/window1.
Keep the three thesis candidates: opportunity/execution measurement, background
trade-offs, and context/cost of agent representations. This horizon pilot belongs
as a bounded diagnostic/null plus an explicitly incomplete comparison.

Next PROPOSED work is a small controlled information/behaviour study with fresh
opportunities, positive/downside/no-signal conditions and actual executions.
For a headline claim, hold the pre-event state/economic change fixed and vary
headline availability prospectively; simulator seeds alone do not match LLM
states. Use the corrected simple agent and specified equal-resource rule
policies first. Isolate one trait on the same state if making a trait claim.
Retain A/B/C as selectable mechanisms, without multiplying every new question
by three. At most one justified background sensitivity for a leading result.
Do not add another qualification sweep, provider switch, reflection default,
episode rewrite or larger horizon grid just to obtain an expected sign.
